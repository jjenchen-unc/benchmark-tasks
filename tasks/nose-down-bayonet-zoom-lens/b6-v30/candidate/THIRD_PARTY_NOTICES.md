No third-party meshes or textures are bundled. All submitted task geometry is
procedurally generated from the B6 task-specific primitives. Native ASSETS.md
states the original native Panda assets use the robosuite/MIT notice; those
assets are not included in this port. Full original asset/provenance statements
are retained in the readiness snapshot, with their exact SHA256 hashes.

The genuine YAM robot and standard station are external installed SDK assets,
not copied into this package; their existing SDK/I2RT license and source notices
continue to apply. Versions and source provenance are recorded separately.
