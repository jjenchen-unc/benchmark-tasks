## Robot handling fixture

Two removable training handles each use a rubber-coated 36 mm diameter pot magnet, a short stud and a flat-sided 28 × 32 × 36 mm rubberized grip with its center 49 mm from the plate plane. They model off-the-shelf magnetic sheet-handling aids and can be made from a threaded pot magnet and a flat-sided grip. Their centers lie 132 mm left and right of center and 7 mm below the cover midpoint, on solid strips outside the breaker openings. The model rigidly attaches both handles to the plate for the trial. Physical replication requires measuring slip under shear on the actual coated sheet. A pull rating measured normal to a magnet does not establish its vertical shear capacity. [Industrial magnetic sheet handlers](https://www.magnetics.com/products/sheet-handlers).

The modeled sheet and folds weigh 2.128 kg, and the two 85 g fixture handles bring the total to 2.298 kg. A balanced two-arm carry puts about 1.15 kg on each arm before dynamic loading. During the transfer to one hand the policy requires measured upward support from the tub of at least half the assembly weight. The reference completed the simulated transfer and lock-up. Dynamic wrist loads and magnetic retention on the physical coated plate still require measurement.
