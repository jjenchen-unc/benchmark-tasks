native variant is deleted or redefined, and none is counted as an additional
Arena-ready task. The exact source snapshot used by the offline export retains:

- A_take_out_B: open the right green case and stand its instrument outside.
- DL-T1: release both left hooks, open its lid past60degrees, keep the
  instrument inside and upright, then20second hands-off hold.
- DL-T2: close left lid, attach/tension both hooks, flatten levers, return
  catches home, then20second hands-off hold.
- DL-T3: transfer to the brown right case, release hooks, open past60degrees
  and stand its instrument outside, then20second hands-off hold.
- DL-T4: brown case open past60degrees at least once, reclose, attach/tension
  both hooks, flatten levers and return catches home, then20second hands-off.

All original numeric partial stages and source safety logic remain in the
unchanged native package. Native variants have not been silently converted
into the narrower representative task.
