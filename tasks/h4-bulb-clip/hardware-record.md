# GSX250R bulb holder measurement record

This blank record accompanies the contact model. Send the identification and photo request to an OEM Suzuki dismantler or Suzuki parts department that has the complete 2018 US GSX250R L8 headlamp in hand. A bench technician with the assembly and a two-axis force measurement can complete the displacement record. Existing catalogue and service references are in the [task description](README.md).

**Identification and photographs.** Record the assembly label, 35100-20K30 or its service replacement 35100-20K31, bulb cap, and condition of the fixed catch and hinge. Photograph the rear square-on with the wide locating slot up, the hinge keeper installed and a millimetre scale in the flange plane. Take a side view through the catch at flange height with a second scale in that plane. Include one image each with wire open, just clear of the lip, and released under the lip. The kit clasp at the hinge and the operating end at the catch have different functions. [Suzuki service bulletin, pp. 6–9](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2020/RCMN-20V612-5758.pdf)

**Force measurements.** Clamp the unpowered housing at its normal mounting points. Seat the bulb. Mark the contact point on the operating end and use the same point in every trial. Zero with the probe clear. Record axial force along the insertion axis and lateral force along the direction that clears the catch. Synchronize the orthogonal video and force readings. Sample each 0.5 mm of travel and at engagement or release, across five engage/release cycles. Record installed axial flange preload in a separate run with a load-sensing cap surrogate preserving the cap seating and rear contact planes; identify any stack-height error. A pull on the operating end does not measure flange preload.

Overlay the five curves against the simulated model in the README. Report the spread of peak force, travel required to clear and returned preload. The nominal model uses about 2 mm lateral clearance motion, with 4 N initial resistance plus 1.6 N/mm; the actual assembly values are open fields.
