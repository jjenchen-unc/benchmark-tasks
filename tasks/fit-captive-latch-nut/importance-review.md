The substantive combination is selecting a close metric/imperial open-end fit for an unmarked12.70mm captive nut, avoiding irreversible clearance/torque-based rounding, releasing a passive clamp and opening its arm, then proving contact-free success for20s. The task shares ordinary grasping, tool alignment and rotation with existing benchmarks. New colours, robot placement, the tool rack, and generic wrench use are not novelty arguments.
