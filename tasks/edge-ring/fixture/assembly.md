# Maintenance replica

The simulation places the chamber and unloaded ring stand on the station centreline, 405 mm and 120 mm forward of the line between the two arm bases. This placement lets the two arms pinch opposite points on the ring and lower it past the full 127 mm rim.

In the Brev reference run the robot cleared this continuous rim, centred the seated ring to 0.143 mm and withdrew both hands for a score of 1.0 at step 1,001. The forced-press run peaked at 27.08 N with 1.27 s above the limit and scored 0. Use the station coordinates above when reproducing those runs.

DXF units are mm, centre is the chuck axis, +x defines the 0° dowel. Import at 1:1 and check the following dimensions before cutting. Laser/waterjet outlines are finished dimensions, apply kerf compensation externally.

Fit a three-axis force platform beneath all stationary ring supports including the stand and the complete lift drive. Isolate the pendant and the robots' bases. Acquire synchronized XYZ at ≥1 kHz. Tare unloaded, characterize the empty lift over a full cycle and subtract that baseline at the same lift position/direction. Calibrate each axis with known loads and check cross-axis response. Sum the simultaneous force vectors before taking magnitude. Video and contact markers must show the hand loading the ring with no direct arm contact on the instrumented fixture; ambiguous direct loads are unscorable. During robot–ring contact, a resultant strictly above ring weight + 10 N continuously for 50 ms vetoes the trial. Verify operation with a known load slightly above and below threshold and a timed pulse. Keep the raw trace and synchronized video. The force criterion cannot be inferred from downward motion alone.

For the shop-built route, cut a witness slot in the centre offcut first. Choose the pilot allowance from its measured taper and profile error so both cut edges remain inside the finished slot. Use a calibrated measuring microscope or optical comparator referenced to the ring centre and the first slot. Inspect both faces of all three slots for width (4.27–4.33 mm), length (8.00 mm nominal) and centre error (≤0.05 mm). A 4.00 mm pin can pass a miscut slot. Record the optical dimensions and dry-fit every slot simultaneously on the finished base before using the ring.
